## Arms

- `q6-copies` (topics): the handed entry carries the mentioning topics' titles and names as copies rather than references
- `r1-pruned` (topics): the copies arm with the board's mention universe bounded to its 50 most recently filed members
- `r1-lazy` (topics): the copies arm where the editor completes over a session copy the open verb takes, so nothing reads the universe at startup
- `r3-one-entry` (topics): everything the topic reads of its board in one handed entry: its name, its backlinks, the board's name and a bounded universe, all as values, filled one entry per topic
- `r5-best` (topics): the two halves that measured best: entries filled one per topic, and the universe bounded and left as its own input rather than copied into every entry
- `r6-table-copies` (topics): no handed reference: the board publishes its entries as one table of values and each topic finds its own row by the name it stores

## Time

`buildMs` files N topics and makes the mentions, and is the rig's cost rather
than a product figure; `deferred` means the board's outputs were demanded once
at the end of the build instead of throughout. `compileB` compiles the program
in the fresh runtime, before the start. `pull` is `cell.pull()` on the topic's
schema-less result cell, which is what `PiecesController.startPiece` does after
`runtime.start`.
