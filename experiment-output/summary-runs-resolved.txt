## Arms

- `q3-index` (topics): the board indexes one entry per topic by name; the topic looks its own up with `lookup`, and holds its name as a stored value
- `q4-handed` (topics): the create mints each topic's entry document and hands the topic that document; a board lift fills it
- `q6-copies` (topics): the handed entry carries the mentioning topics' titles and names as copies rather than references
- `q7-board-name` (topics): the copies arm, plus one reference to the board, through which the topic reads the collection's declared name

## Time

`buildMs` files N topics and makes the mentions, and is the rig's cost rather
than a product figure; `deferred` means the board's outputs were demanded once
at the end of the build instead of throughout. `compileB` compiles the program
in the fresh runtime, before the start. `pull` is `cell.pull()` on the topic's
schema-less result cell, which is what `PiecesController.startPiece` does after
`runtime.start`.
